LineDraw — Privacy Policy

Vexlo · Last updated: August 1, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Line Draw mobile application ("the app"), published by Vexlo. We designed Line Draw to collect as little data as possible.

Data we collect

Line Draw itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Your game progress, star ratings, and settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's Advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data

This collection is performed by Google under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings, and you can remove banner and interstitial ads entirely via the app's one-time "Remove Ads" purchase.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase state (ads removed or not) is stored locally on your device and with your Google Play account for restore purposes.

Children

Line Draw is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
