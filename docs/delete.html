LineDraw — Request Data Deletion

Vexlo · Line Draw (Google Play)

This page explains how to request deletion of data related to the Line Draw app by Vexlo.

How to request deletion

  1. Email info@vexlo.co.uk from the address associated with your Google Play account if possible.
  2. Use the subject line: Line Draw data deletion request.
  3. Include your Google Play email (or Advertising ID if you know it) and confirm you want your Line Draw–related data deleted.

We aim to respond within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • On your device: Game progress, stars, and settings are stored only locally. You can delete them anytime by clearing Line Draw’s app data in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Advertising data: Ads are served by Google AdMob. We do not store AdMob data ourselves. We will process your request and, where applicable, help you exercise deletion/opt-out rights for advertising identifiers. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device’s Google settings.
  • Purchases: “Remove Ads” purchase records are held by Google Play. We cannot erase Google’s purchase history; Google manages that under your Google account.

What may be retained

  • Emails you send us about the request, kept only as long as needed to fulfil and document the request (typically up to 90 days), then deleted.
  • Data Google retains under its own policies (AdMob / Play), which you can manage through your Google account settings.
